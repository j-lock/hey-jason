# Hey, Jason — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Hey, Jason is a personal reminder app built for and used only by its owner, Jason Lockerbie.
It is not sold, advertised, or offered to the public.

## What the app stores

- Reminders, notes, places, shopping list items and any photos or files you attach.
- This information is stored on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive sync, a copy is saved
  in a hidden app folder in **your own** Google Drive so your phones stay in step.

## Google Drive access

- The app only requests the `drive.appdata` permission. This lets it read and write its **own**
  hidden folder in your Drive. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
- Your data is never sent to the developer or any other server.

## Location

- If you allow it, your approximate location is used only to show nearby places first when you
  search for a location. It is not stored or shared.
- Place searches are sent to the free OpenStreetMap search service (Photon) to find matching places.

## Sharing

- No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app contains no analytics or tracking.

## Deleting your data

- Uninstalling the app removes data from that phone.
- To remove synced data, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Hey, Jason →
  Delete hidden app data.

## Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
